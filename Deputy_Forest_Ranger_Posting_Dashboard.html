Reconciliation method and source cautions
Sources: “Deputy Ranger Gradation List.xlsx” and “Compiled Dr.FR List.xlsx”.
  • Matching was performed within each normalized Division first. Titles such as Sri/Smt/FR and “(Cg)” were ignored; clear spelling and omitted-middle-name variants were reviewed.
  • All logical posting records are represented. Gradation officers have no reliable Posting Return match and are marked “Present only in Gradation List”.
  • Duration is calculated to 29 Aug 2026 from the Beat posting date. For Gradation-only records, Division Date is used as a clearly marked fallback.
  • Four Working Plan entries reconstructed from split source rows retain both original Excel row references. Seven ambiguous Excel dates were interpreted day-first, and one malformed date was read as 11 Aug 2026; every correction is noted in the detail view.
  • Two different employee IDs share the name Suvankar Mandal. Their assignments are explained and flagged in the detail view.
