Reconciliation method and source cautions
Sources: “Forest Ranger Gradation List(1).xlsx” and “Complied Forest Ranger.xlsx”.
  • Matching was performed within each normalized Division first. Titles such as Sri/Smt/FR and “(Cg)” were ignored; clear spelling and omitted-middle-name variants were reviewed.
  • All logical posting records are represented. Gradation officers have no reliable Posting Return match and are marked “Present only in Gradation List”.
  • Duration is calculated to 29 Aug 2026 from the Range joining date. For Gradation-only records, Division Date is used as a clearly marked fallback.
  • Rows reconstructed from split/merged source layouts retain their original Excel row references in the detail view. Dates later than the snapshot remain visible but are excluded from numeric duration filters.
  • Two different employee IDs share the name Sukanta Ojha. Their assignment is explained and flagged in the detail view.
